The Frying Pan

207 12th Avenue, Pier 66 · Chelsea
★★★★☆ 4.3 (2,600+ reviews)

A floating bar & restaurant on a restored 1929 lightship at Pier 66. The vessel sank in 1986, was raised, and rebuilt into one of NYC’s best outdoor waterfront spots. Casual vibes, sunset views over the Hudson, and ice-cold drinks on the water.

What people say
“One of the best outdoor bars in NYC, located directly on the Hudson River.”
“The food is absolutely amazing and the drinks are priced super reasonable.”
#1 Ordered
Lobster Roll
Fresh lobster on a buttered split-top roll. The waterfront classic, paired with their famous frozen sangria.

Swingers

35 W 29th St · NoMad
★★★★☆ 4.4 (990+ reviews)

A 23,000 sq ft underground crazy golf venue from London. Three 9-hole courses, six cocktail bars, live DJs, and gourmet street food—all themed as a 1920s English country golf club. Personal caddies deliver drinks to you on the course. 21+ only.

What people say
“I’ve never had more fun at a bar in my adult life. Super cute decor and expertly curated music.”
“Such a fun place! Food, drinks, service, a live DJ—all great!”
#1 Ordered
Frozen Cocktails & Quesabirria
Grab the Full Swing package—9 holes bundled with a $50 food & drink credit. Frozen cocktails from the bar plus quesabirria tacos from Fonda.

Peter Luger Steak House

178 Broadway · Williamsburg, Brooklyn
★★★★☆ 4.4 (13,500+ reviews)

Brooklyn’s legendary steakhouse since 1887. Cash only, no menus needed—you’re here for the Porterhouse. Zagat rated it #1 steakhouse in NYC for 30 consecutive years. Old-school German beer hall atmosphere with famously gruff waiters and wood-paneled walls.

What people say
“The Porterhouse for two is arguably the best steak you will ever eat in your entire life.”
“A quintessential NYC experience. The sizzling platter presentation is iconic.”
Legendary #1
Porterhouse for Two
USDA Prime dry-aged Porterhouse, sliced tableside and served sizzling on a hot plate with their signature house steak sauce. Cash only.
7:00 PM — ALT
Option 2

Keens Steakhouse

72 W 36th St · Midtown
★★★★★ 4.6 (9,700+ reviews)

NYC’s legendary steakhouse since 1885. Mahogany walls, 50,000 clay smoking pipes on the ceilings, and a Pipe Club roster that includes Teddy Roosevelt, Babe Ruth, and Albert Einstein. The Infatuation gave it a 9.0—“Nowhere in Manhattan is history more palpable.” Stays in Manhattan—8-minute walk from Swingers.

What people say
“Hands down best steakhouse in the city with wonderful service.”
“Consistently serves remarkably tender, flavorful, and perfectly charred Prime cuts.”
Legendary #1
The Mutton Chop
A 26-ounce, 2-inch-thick saddle chop of year-old lamb, seared at 1,000°F. On the menu since 1935. Served with mint jelly and sautéed escarole.
