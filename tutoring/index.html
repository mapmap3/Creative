About Us

A family-owned virtual tutoring company

Connecting Concepts Tutoring (CCT) is a family-owned virtual tutoring company founded in 2020 to help students of all ages succeed. We provide personalized, one-on-one tutoring for all K-12 subjects as well as SAT and ACT test preparation.

We at CCT firmly believe that you determine your future. You can meet your goals and we are here to help you get there. Our mission is to connect students with quality tutors in a safe, supportive, and affordable environment.
